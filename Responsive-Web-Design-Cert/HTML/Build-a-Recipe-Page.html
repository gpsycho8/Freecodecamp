No-bake Oreo Cheesecake

This No Bake Oreo Cheesecake is the ultimate cookies and cream dessert. With a crunchy Oreo crust and a creamy cheesecake filling, it’s so easy to make! You don’t even need to turn the oven on.

Ingredients

  • Oreos: 26 cookies (16 crushed for crust, 10 chopped for filling).
  • Butter: 4 tablespoons of melted unsalted butter.
  • Cream Cheese: 2 packages (8 ounces each), softened to room temperature.
  • Powdered Sugar: 1 cup (holds the structure better than granulated sugar).
  • Vanilla Extract: 1 teaspoon.
  • Heavy Whipping Cream: 1 cup, chilled.

Instructions

  1. Make Crust: Mix 16 finely crushed Oreos with melted butter. Press firmly into a 9-inch pan. Freeze for 10 minutes.
  2. Whip Cream: Beat heavy cream in a bowl until stiff peaks form. Set aside.
  3. Make Filling: Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla in a separate bowl until smooth.
  4. Fold Ingredients: Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture. Fold in 6 chopped Oreos.
  5. Assemble: Spread filling evenly over the frozen crust. Top with remaining 4 chopped Oreos.
  6. Chill: Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (or overnight) until completely firm.
